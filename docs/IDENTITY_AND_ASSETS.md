## Separate rights review

Before public release, an owner or qualified reviewer must:

1. Search relevant trademark registers and marketplaces for the approved name
   and logo in target jurisdictions.
2. Trace every shipped icon, illustration, screenshot cover, bundled pattern
   and other visual/text asset to a license or original-work record.
3. Confirm attribution, notice, source-offer and copyleft obligations for the
   distributed artifact, not only the source repository.
4. Replace assets with uncertain provenance; do not rely on repository presence
   as proof of publication rights.
5. Store the signed review/evidence outside the build artifact and link it from
   the release record.

This inventory is engineering evidence, not legal clearance.
